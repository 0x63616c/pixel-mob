A LITTLE CROWD GOES A LONG WAY

Say it with a mob.

Pixel Mob turns your words into a living pixel-art message. The crowd wanders, a leader calls “Mob, assemble!”, and everyone runs into place. It’s a tiny surprise, made just for them.

  1. 01

    Write your words

    A birthday wish. An inside joke. A very important “HI”. You get 32 characters. The mob handles the rest.

  2. 02

    Copy your link or use your phone’s share sheet. The preview keeps the message a surprise.

  3. 03

    Watch them assemble

    Your friend opens the link, taps to reveal, and the mob spells it out. Confetti very much included.

Can anyone read my message?

Your message is scrambled into the link, not saved in a database. This is a surprise, not encryption: anyone with the link can reveal it. Please don’t use it for private information.

What can the mob spell?

Up to 32 letters, numbers, spaces and supported punctuation, plus hearts. Accented letters are simplified, and we’ll tell you if a character can’t be used.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Pixel Mob is made for modern phone and desktop browsers, with touch controls, keyboard support and a reduced-motion option that follows your device’s preference.
