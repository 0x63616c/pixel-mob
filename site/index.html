A LITTLE CROWD GOES A LONG WAY

Say it with a mob.

Pixel Mob turns your words into a living pixel-art message. The crowd wanders, a leader calls “Mob, assemble!”, and everyone runs into place. It’s a tiny surprise, made just for them.

  1. 01

    Write your words

    A birthday wish. An inside joke. A very important “HI”. Add a ❤️ or a 🎂, up to four scenes, a theme or a countdown. The mob handles the rest.

  2. 02

    Send the surprise

    Copy your link or use your phone’s share sheet. The preview keeps the message a surprise.

  3. 03

    Watch them assemble

    Your friend taps to reveal, the mob spells it out, then they react, save it as a GIF or video, or reply with a mob of their own.

Can anyone read my message?

Your message is scrambled into the link, not saved in a database. This is a surprise, not encryption: anyone with the link can reveal it. Please don’t use it for private information.

What can the mob spell?

Up to 32 letters, numbers, spaces and symbols per scene, plus 20 pixel drawings such as ❤️ 🎂 🎉 ⭐ 💍 🎄 🎃 and 🏆. Accented letters are simplified, and we’ll tell you if a character can’t be used.

Can I save it or schedule it?

Yes. Whoever opens it can save the reveal as a GIF or video, made on their own device with a small pixelmob.io mark. Set “Opens at” and the link shows a live countdown until the moment you pick.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Pixel Mob is made for modern phone and desktop browsers, with touch controls, keyboard support and a reduced-motion option that follows your device’s preference.
