# Needed art (void-floor expansion)

New props and sheets not yet in `game/assets/art/atlas.json`. Sizes are tiles at 16px. Style: retro pixel, muted Plenum palette, no glass walls. One line each.

- **ping-pong-table** — 6×3 table with paddles and a ball; net is a 1px stripe.
- **vending-machine** — 2×3 machine, dark teal door, one coil window, lit button strip.
- **aquarium-tank** — 8×4 glass tank; looping swim cycles for six small fish, rising bubbles, a castle; **night glow** (cool wash + brighter bubbles) as a second state or overlay.
- **fish-food-shelf** — 2×2 shelf with a labelled tin and a scoop.
- **nap-pod** — 2×2 capsule, open and occupied (sleeping silhouette) states; soft shadow.
- **mystery-door** — 2×2 free-standing door, no wall; slightly too white, dark gap.
- **goat-fence** — 1×1 fence segments + a corner post; east side can be a gap.
- **hay-bale** — 1×1 or 2×1 tidy bale for the pen.
- **goat-bed** — 2×2 straw pad (the Goat already has a sheet; this is the bed prop).
- **plant-wall** — 1×2 and 1×1 foliage tiles that tile into a vertical hedge.
- **mail-slot** — 1×1 or 6×1 row of pigeonholes, some with letters.
- **mail-counter** — 6×1 counter top, paper pile optional.
- **package** — 1×1 wrapped box, one typo'd label variant.
- **it-desk** — 4×1 bench with a CRT already in the atlas; maybe a spare cable pile.
- **archive-shelf / bookshelf-wide** — 12×1 and 6×1 packed shelves (Year One binding colour).
- **whiteboard-full** — 4×1 whiteboard packed with tiny unreadable arrows (if current `whiteboard` is blank).
- **intercom** — 1×1 ceiling or pillar speaker for “the intercom is thinking”.
- **fish-food-flake** — 1×1 decal of flakes on water, for the feeding duty.
- **crt-flicker** — 1-frame glitch overlay for an existing CRT, or a second crt state.
- **portrait-blink** — 1-frame closed-eye swap for the Founder portrait.

Existing atlas props (desks, CRTs, chairs, mugs, cactus, elevator, shrine, etc.) stay; click descriptions live in `objects.catalog` and do not require new art.
