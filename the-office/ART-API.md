# Pixel Office art: sheet and atlas contract (for the Bevy engine)

All art lives in `game/assets/art/`. It is generated by `python3 tools/art/build.py`, which reads `game/assets/data/company.json`, so re-run that after data changes. Don't hand-edit the PNGs.
Load every texture with **nearest sampling** (`ImagePlugin::default_nearest()`). 1 tile = 16 px. Camera zoom must be an integer (1x–6x) and positions should be snapped to whole logical pixels.
The single source of truth is `atlas.json`. Every number below is also in it.

## 1. People: `people/<person_id>.png` (one sheet per person in company.json)
- Look up a person with `atlas.people.by_person[id]`. `atlas.people.kinds[id]` is staff, receptionist, cleaner or security. `atlas.people.presets` lists generic visitor sheets (`people/p00..p21.png`).
- **Cell 24×32 px.** The grid has `cols = 8` (unused cells are transparent) and **88 rows** (22 anims × 4 dirs; round 2 appended 6 anims at the end, so rows 0–63 are unchanged). Row order is `atlas.people.rows`: each anim in the order below, × directions `S, N, E, W`. So `row = anim_index*4 + dir_index`.
- **Foot anchor: (12, 30)** inside the cell (feet bottom row). Put this pixel on the actor's world position, so the sprite top-left = `pos - (12, 30)`.
- **Z-sort:** by foot y (larger y draws later). All seated anims assume the chair is under the person; see chairs below.

- **Roles read by silhouette:** Facilities (`cleaner`) wear grey jumpsuits and caps and push `facilities_cart`. The Floor Supervisor (`security`) wears a near-black suit with tie and badge and uses the flashlight anim at night. The receptionist (`bea`) wears a headset.
- **Look mapping** from company.json is in `build.py:from_data`. Shirt and pants colours snap to the 70s palette. Outfits map tee→collared shirt, blazer→suit, hoodie→turtleneck, vest→sweater vest, uniform→jumpsuit. Accessories cover lanyard→badge, glasses, sunglasses, headphones, airpods and earrings, plus beard/mustache from `extra`. A `beanie` becomes a knit cap. The resolved looks are in `atlas.people.looks`.

## 2. Animals: `goat.png`, `biscuit.png` (corgi)
- Cell **24×24**, cols 4, rows `idle_S, idle_N, idle_E, idle_W, walk_S, walk_N, walk_E, walk_W`, foot anchor **(12, 21)**. idle is 4 frames at 3 fps (chew / pant); walk is 4 frames at 7 fps. Draw `shadow_goat` under both. The goat wears its lanyard.

## 3. Props, tiles, FX and UI: `props.png` + `atlas.props[name]`
Each entry has `frames: [[x,y,w,h], …]` (rects in props.png) and `anchor: [ax, ay]` (pixel placed on the world position, normally the ground-contact point, which is also the **sort y**). Optional fields:
`fps`, `states` (named frame lists), `seats` / `crt` / `keyboard` / `items` (offsets from the anchor), `float` (hover it 10–14 px above its shadow and bob ±1 px at ~0.25 Hz), `slice` (9-slice border px), `tile` (16×16 tile, anchor top-left), `text_rect` (where to overlay Bevy text), and `note`.
**Soft contact shadows are baked into props.** People and animals use the separate `shadow_person` / `shadow_goat` (skip it when seated).

- **Desks:** `desk_pod4` (70×50; four walnut desks with a low teal fabric divider). Its `seats` give the seated FOOT positions: two north seats face S, two south seats face N. Its `crt` entries give monitor positions (north desks use `crt_back`, south desks `crt_front`). There are also `keyboard` and `items` (belongings) slots, and `desk` for a single desk.
  - Draw order for a pod: north workers + their `chair_s_*` → pod → CRTs/keyboards/belongings (sort y = pod y + 0.4..0.5) → south workers → their `chair_n_*` (drawn AFTER the person, covering their lower back).
- **CRTs:** `crt_front` has 6 frames at 4 fps of mysterious scrolling numbers. `crt_back` is the beige back with a phosphor spill. At night, add an additive green glow (rgba(80,200,130,~0.22)) at the screen.
- **Chairs:** `chair_{s,n,e,w}_{orange,mustard,teal,avocado,cream}` are Eames-style shells. Anchor = seat foot point; place it at the person's foot y + 1.
- **Meeting tables** (no walls; pair with `outline_*` floor tiles, a floating `room_tag` and `sign_shadow`):
  - `table_booth` (1 seat)
  - `table_huddle` (4)
  - `table_team` (8)
  - `table_boardroom` (14)
  - all-hands kit: `lectern`, `bench` (4 seats each, facing N) and `projector_screen` (floating, animated chart).

  Seats are offsets from the table centre. North seats face S and are tucked under the table edge so their legs are hidden.
- **Elevator:**
  - `elevator` door frames are 0 = closed to 5 = open. States: `closed`, `opening`, `open`, `closing` at 8 fps. The interior is warm-lit.
  - `elevator_rise` frames are 0 = sunk into the white to 8 = fully risen (10 fps). Play it reversed to sink.
  - New hires step out at anchor y-6 and walk forward.
- **Other furniture:**
  - `coffee_station`: `states.ok` [0,1,2] has steam; `states.broken` [3,4] has a red light and smoke.
  - `water_cooler` (bubbles), `filing_cabinet` and `filing_cabinet_avocado`.
  - `snake_plant`, `snake_plant_terracotta` and `plant_tall`.
  - `founder_shrine`: original generic portrait, flickering candles.
  - `desk_lamp`: states `off`/`on`; turn it on at night with a warm glow.
  - `facilities_cart`.
- **Belongings:** `mug_{orange,mustard,teal,cream}`, `photo_frame`, `figurine`, `stress_ball`, `cactus`, `nameplate`, `papers`. Reward tchotchkes are `eraser` and `caricature`; party items are `melon` and `waffle`. Mix 1–2 per desk from the pod `items` slots; vary them per person so desks feel personal.
- **Desk phones:** `phone` and `phone_ring` (12 fps shake; use it for the lone ringing phone in the void).
- **Signage** (floating, all original copy):
  - `sign_0..6` (cream boards): ALIGNMENT IS ITS OWN REWARD / PLEASE ENJOY EACH METRIC EQUALLY / WE REFINE. WE ALIGN. WE ARE GRATEFUL. / THE NUMBERS ARE GRATEFUL FOR YOU / WELLNESS SESSION / BREAK ROOM / GRATITUDE ASSEMBLY.
  - `sign_dept_a`/`_b` (dark terminal boards): OUTPUT CALIBRATION / PRODUCT REFINEMENT.
  - `sign_blank_{40,64,96}` are for your own text, which goes in `text_rect` (Silkscreen 8 px, colour #2a3a36).
  - `room_tag` is a small dark tag with green text.
- **Floor tiles:**
  - Carpet islands use the 9-slice `carpet_{tl,t,tr,l,c,r,bl,b,br}`. Add `carpet_shadow` in the row just below the island's bottom edge.
  - Meeting outlines use the dashed hairline 9-slice `outline_*` (draw only the border tiles).
  - `void_grid` is 64×64 and tiles infinitely. Fade its alpha out with distance from the camera centre.
- **FX:**
  - `fx_zzz` (sleepers, anchor above head)
  - `fx_confetti` (8 frames, no loop)
  - `fx_sparkle`
  - `fx_steam`
  - `fx_flashlight_{E,W,S,N}`: warm cone. The anchor is the hand; draw it additively, at night only.
  - `select_ring`: dashed green ellipse at the feet, 2 frames.
- **Emotes:** `emote_*` are 15×17 badges whose anchor is the tail tip. Pop them 26 px above the foot with a 3 px rise. Available: exclaim, question, zzz, heart, coffee, party, skull, angry, sweat, idea, eyes, music, numbers, check, dots, sparkle, goat, melon, waffle, alarm.
- **Bubbles:** `bubble`, `bubble_memo`, `bubble_whisper` are 9-slices with `slice = 5`. Each has a matching `*_tail`, centred under the bubble and overlapping its bottom border by 1 px. `bubble_typing` is a 3-frame "…" indicator.
  - Text is **Silkscreen 8 px**, colour = the border colour (#2a2622; memo #7cf2a0). Use 5 px padding left/right and 3 px top, so the bubble is 15 px tall per line and wraps at ~120 px.
  - Pop-in: scale 0 → 1.15 → 1 over 0.18 s; pop-out: 1 → 0 over 0.18 s. Rasterise the text at 1x, then scale by an integer so it stays crisp.
- **UI panels:** `panel_memo` (green-on-black terminal 9-slice) and `panel_beige` (beige plastic 9-slice). `scanlines` is a 4×4 tile for CRT/UI panels only.

## 4. Extra textures
- `vignette.png` (256×256): stretch it over the screen at about 0.9 alpha. This is the only texture that may use linear filtering.

## 5. Fonts: `game/assets/fonts/`
- `Silkscreen-Regular.ttf` / `-Bold.ttf`: world text, bubbles and signs. Use 8 px, or exact multiples of it.
- `VT323-Regular.ttf`: memo feed and terminal UI at 16/19/24 px.
- Both are SIL OFL; the licences are next to the fonts.

## 6. Palette and lighting
- **Void:** #fbfaf7 at the centre to #efeee9 at the edges, with a radial falloff and a faint grid. Add a white haze across the top ~46 px as the horizon fade.
- **Base colours:**
  - carpet teal #2f6f68 (rim #43867c, edge #22524d)
  - walnut #8e5b37 / #a9734a / #6c4329
  - beige plastic #d9cdaf
  - CRT #0c261c, glow #7cf2a0
  - brass #c9a03a
  - mustard #c9a03a, burnt orange #c0622f, avocado #7a8a3a, cream #efe6d0
  - outline = each colour darkened ×0.5 (selective outline, 1 px)
- **Global light cycle:** multiply the whole frame by `rgb(255-70n, 255-52n, 255-28n)`, where n = night 0..1. That takes the white to a pale blue-grey. Then additively draw:
  - CRT glows (green, r≈22 px, 0.22·n)
  - lit desk lamps (warm #ffcd78, r≈20, 0.28·n)
  - the elevator interior (warm, r≈34)
  - the supervisor's flashlight cone
- **Void flicker:** for ~0.6 s, overlay rgba(255,255,255,0.75) on random frames.

## 7. Round 2 (data expansion + polish)
Everything below is additive; no round-1 key changed meaning.

### 7.1 Furniture keyed by company.json `type`
For every `layout.furniture[]` (and `layout.void_props[]`) entry, resolve the sprite with the **first key that exists**:
1. `` `${type}_${w}x${h}` `` (size variants, e.g. `table_10x2`, `rug_8x3`, `screen_3x1`, `bookshelf_12x1`, `plant-wall_1x2`, `hay-bale_1x1`)
2. `` `${type}_${facing}` `` (e.g. `desk-chair_up`, `armchair_down`, `chair_left`, `crt_up`)
3. `` `${type}` ``

`build.py` prints `UNRESOLVED types: []` when every data type resolves (it does now, 100%).
- **Anchor = bottom-centre of the footprint**, so world px = `((x + w/2)·16, (y + h)·16)`. That is also the sort y. Each entry has `footprint: [w, h]` and `kind: "furniture"`. Soft contact shadows are baked.
- **Colour variants (optional):** `desk-chair_{up,down,left,right}_{teal,orange,mustard,avocado,cream}`, `armchair_{dir}_{same 5}`, `chair_{n,s,e,w}_{same 5}`, `beanbag_{orange,teal,mustard}`, `plant_{monstera,fern,rubber,succulent}`. Pick one colour per `room_id` / `section_id` / `area` (not per chair) so rooms look curated. The preview hashes the room id.
- **Flat (draw before everything, no sort):** `rug*`, `dog-bed`, `corridor-strip` (a 16×16 tile; repeat it along x for `w` tiles and fade alpha with distance).

### 7.2 Island floors (legend letters)
`isle_<L>_{tl,t,tr,l,c,r,bl,b,br}` are 16×16 tiles (anchor top-left) for `L` ∈ `x k l a n b q g m i p v s`:
- `x` quiet annex (moss)
- `k` kitchen (cream checker lino)
- `l` library (mustard rug)
- `a` archive (warm taupe)
- `n` nap pods (pale sage)
- `b` break (teal with orange geo)
- `q` aquarium (pale aqua terrazzo)
- `g` goat pen (straw)
- `m` mail (grey lino)
- `i` IT (lino with teal checks)
- `p` plant wall (moss)
- `v` offsite / board table (white marble)
- `s` team section

Pick the piece from the 4-neighbours that share the same letter (top/bottom edge first, then left/right). Corners are transparent-rounded and the bottom edge carries a 1 px drop. For `o`, draw only the border pieces of `outline2_*` (corner brackets + faint dots). These replace the older dashed `outline_*`, which still exists.

### 7.3 Light and atmosphere
- **Glows** (`kind: "light"`, `blend: "additive"`): `glow_monitor` (green CRT pool), `glow_lamp` (warm), `glow_fluoro` (cool, 72×44), `glow_aquarium`, `glow_elevator`, `glow_candle`, `glow_small`. Anchor = centre.
  - **Night:** after the multiply pass, draw them with additive blending at alpha 1 (fluoro ~0.55).
  - **Day:** draw them normally (alpha blend) at ~0.35 so islands still read as lit.
- **Fluorescent panels:** `fluoro_panel_32`/`_48` float ~28–30 px above the floor. Use them only over open floor or aisles, never above seated rows, because they read as bars across heads. The preview drops them and keeps only the `glow_fluoro` pool, one per 6 tiles of each section plus the tank, archive and board-visit rooms.
- **Soft shadows:** `shadow_soft_{1,2,3,4,6,8}` are ellipses sized for a footprint width in tiles, for any sprite you add without a baked shadow. `shadow_person_soft` replaces `shadow_person` for standing/walking people (skip it when seated).
- **Atmosphere:**
  - `void_grid_fine` (64×64 tile): cross-hairs + speckle. Fade alpha ≈ `0.9 − dist/(60 tiles)` from the floor centre.
  - `fx_motes` (96×96 tile, 8f @4, additive): tile it in screen space offset by the camera, at alpha ~0.35 by day and 0.5 by night.
  - Then `vignette.png` at 0.55 by day and 0.9 by night.

### 7.6 Smaller signage
- Every data string is pre-baked with a 3×5 pixel font on an 11 px plaque:
  - `signtxt_<sign.id>` for all `layout.signs` (cream plaque, `float`). Draw `sign_shadow_small` on the ground and float the plaque 12 px above it, ±1 px bob.
  - `sectxt_<section.id>` (dark plaque, flat at the section label).
  - `roomtxt_<room.id>` (dark plaque with capacity, at `room.sign`).

  They are about half the height of round-1 signs. Skip a sign whose text equals its room/section name, and nudge overlapping plaques by ±14 px (the preview's `placeL()` does this).
- **Runtime text:** `tiny_font` is a white glyph strip with `glyphs[ch] = [x, w]`, `glyph_h: 5`, `space: 2` and 1 px tracking, covering A–Z, 0–9 and common punctuation plus `· – — ™ → ←`. Tint it at draw time (the preview's `tinyText()`) over `sign_slim_{cream,dark,ghost}` 9-slices (`slice: 3`, 11 px tall).

### 7.7 Suggested engine mapping
- `events.rs` `pet` → anim `pet` (was Wave), `bow` → `bow` (was Tired).
- Fatigue at a desk → `doze`. Long idle → occasionally play `fidget` / `stretch`. Meeting listeners → `nod` between `sit`.
- Runners on the `corridor-strip` → `run` + `fx_sweat`.

## 8. Preview
`art-preview.html` (repo root) renders every sheet straight from `atlas.json` with no build step. Its JS (`prop()`, `person()`, `nine()`, `bubble()`) is a working reference for anchors, draw order, 9-slices and the light cycle.
